Privacy Policy

DUAL: One Mind · Last updated 8 November 2026

DUAL: One Mind has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The leaderboard only needs a random ID for your device, the nickname you choose, and your best score. From version 1.2 the free iPhone and iPad app shows occasional non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, which a one-time purchase removes; the web version has no ads.

The global leaderboard

The leaderboard runs on the developer's own small server, hosted by Cloudflare. When a run ends, the app sends it four things:

  • a random identifier generated on your device the first time you open the app — it is not your name, Apple ID, phone number, or any advertising identifier, and it is only used so that your device owns one row on the board;
  • the nickname you type on the leaderboard screen (or an automatic “Player-XXXX” name if you don't enter one) — it is shown publicly next to your score, so don't use your real name if you'd rather not;
  • your best score and the level you reached.

That is the whole message. The server does not log your IP address and stores nothing else alongside your score. The data is used only to run the leaderboard — never for advertising, never sold, and never used to track you across other apps or websites.

Pre-release test builds of the iPhone and iPad app sent the same items (random identifier, nickname, best score) to Microsoft Azure PlayFab instead, which processed them on the developer's behalf under the Microsoft Privacy Statement and, like any internet service, received your IP address as part of the connection. Later versions no longer use PlayFab.

If you play without an internet connection, the game simply shows the scores saved on your device.

Apple Game Center (optional, iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app also submits your best score and your achievement progress to Apple so they appear in Apple's Games app alongside your friends. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive your Game Center account details. You can sign out, or choose who sees your profile, in your device's Settings → Game Center.

Ads (iPhone and iPad, version 1.2 and later)

The free app occasionally shows a full-screen ad between runs — never during a run, and not in your first few games. Ads are served by Google AdMob and are always requested as non-personalized: they are not based on your past behaviour, and the app never asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency) and never reads your advertising identifier (IDFA). Ad content is limited to the “General” (family-friendly) rating.

To serve, limit and measure ads, Google's SDK still receives technical data such as your IP address, device model and operating system, and app version, and processes it under Google's Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → ADS → AD PRIVACY.

In-app purchase (iPhone and iPad)

The optional one-time “Remove Ads” purchase turns ads off for good (in version 1.1 the same purchase was sold as “Full Game”, unlocking levels 6–10 — every level is free now). It is processed entirely by Apple under your Apple ID. The developer never receives your name, Apple ID, or any payment details — the app only learns whether the purchase is owned. Once ads are removed, the Google ad SDK is not started at all.

What stays on your device

Your settings (control mode, sound, haptics), your highest level, a copy of your scores, a count of finished runs (used to space out ads), and whether ads are removed are kept in the app's local storage and never leave your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Data we do not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no personalized ads, and no tracking
  • No IP address logging on the leaderboard server
  • No usage or diagnostic analytics
  • No photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or tracking permission — the app requests no permissions

Deleting your leaderboard entry

Email chylik.lukas@gmail.com with your nickname and we will remove your entry and its data from the leaderboard.

Third parties

The app contains no analytics or tracking SDKs. Its only third-party SDK is Google AdMob in the free iPhone and iPad app (see Ads above), which is not started for players who removed ads. The leaderboard runs on the developer's own server hosted by Cloudflare; no leaderboard data is shared with anyone else.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It asks for no personal information; children should pick a nickname that isn't their real name.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling any other data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
