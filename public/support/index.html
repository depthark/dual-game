How to play

Two dots fall through an endless run of gates, each in its own lane. Steer both through their gaps. Clip a wall with either dot and the run ends.

  • TWO HANDS — each thumb drives its own dot.
  • ONE HAND — one thumb spreads and gathers both dots together.

Switch between them in Settings → CONTROL.

Leaderboard

Everyone shares one global leaderboard. Open SCORES and type a nickname in the YOU field (3–14 characters; each nickname can only be used by one player). No account or login is needed — your device keeps its own row and only your best score counts.

Without an internet connection the board shows the scores saved on your device; your best score is sent the next time you play online. On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, your scores also appear in Apple's Games app.

To have your entry removed, email us with your nickname.

Sound and haptics

Both can be turned off in the app's Settings → AUDIO.
